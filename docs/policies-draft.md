# Policy drafts — not approved for publication

## Delivery
Confirm dispatch times, destinations, delivery charges and courier service before checkout. Give customers the total price before purchase and send an order confirmation on a durable medium. Send tracking details when dispatched. Do not promise unverified delivery dates.

## Returns and cancellations
For UK distance sales, consumers generally have 14 days after receipt to notify cancellation and another 14 days to return goods. Confirm applicable exceptions for genuinely personalised commissions; being an original painting does not itself remove cancellation rights. Faulty or misdescribed goods remain subject to statutory rights. Confirm return address, return postage responsibilities, refund process and cancellation form before publication.

## Privacy
Identify the actual adult-controlled legal seller/data controller and contact address. Explain what order, enquiry and newsletter data is collected, purposes and lawful bases, service providers, transfers, retention, individual rights and ICO complaint rights. Marketing consent must be separate, optional and withdrawable. Do not publish children's personal contact details or home/school information. Do not enable non-essential tracking without appropriate consent.

## Required decisions
Legal seller name and geographic business address; support email; delivery destinations, prices and timings; returns address; data processors and retention; newsletter platform; cookie usage. Obtain review before publication. These are working drafts, not legal advice or completed compliance.
